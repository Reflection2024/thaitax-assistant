# Payroll and social security

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Re-verify before use (see `_index.md`). For arithmetic use `scripts/thaitax_calc.py`; its rates live in `scripts/rates.json`.

## Social security (ประกันสังคม)

- 5% employee and 5% employer, on monthly wages up to a ceiling of **THB 17,500** from 1 Jan 2026 (THB 875 maximum each side), with a floor of THB 1,650. The ceiling rises in 2029 and again in 2032.
- Form SPS 1-10 and payment are due the **15th** of the next month. Late payment carries a 2% surcharge per month. **Rounding** (SSO form instructions): each person's contribution is rounded to the baht, with 50 satang or more rounded up and less than 50 satang dropped; the employer pays the same rounded amount. Ceiling THB 17,500 (max 875) for 2026–2028, confirmed in the Royal Gazette; floor THB 1,650. The employee may deduct up to THB 10,500 a year in their personal tax.
- Register a new employee within 30 days of hire. Foreign staff with a work permit are covered.

## Payroll

- **Minimum wage** (Wage Committee Notification No. 14, in force since 1 Jul 2025 and still current in 2026; no newer notification as of Nov 2026): THB 400/day in Bangkok, Phuket, Chachoengsai, Chonburi, Rayong and **Koh Samui district**; **THB 400/day nationwide for hotels of types 2, 3 and 4** (type 1, rooms only and up to 50 rooms, is excluded) and for licensed entertainment venues; the rest of **Surat Thani, including Koh Phangan, THB 352/day** for other businesses. Re-verify every January.
- **Overtime:** 1.5x on a working day. Holiday work is 1x extra for staff entitled to holiday pay, otherwise 2x. Overtime on a holiday is 3x. Hourly rate = monthly wage ÷ (30 × normal daily hours).
- **Severance** (Labour Protection Act s.118, amended by Act No. 7, 2019; verified): 30 days' last wage after 120 days of service, 90 days after 1 year, 180 after 3 years, 240 after 6, 300 after 10, **400 after 20 years**. Show the figure for information only; the decision and payment go through the owner's lawyer.
- A foreigner needs a valid work permit for the employer and the role. Do not stage pay for anyone whose permit status is unknown.
