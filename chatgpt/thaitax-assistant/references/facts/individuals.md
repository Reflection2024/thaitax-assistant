# Individuals and foreign income

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Re-verify before use (see `_index.md`). For arithmetic use `scripts/thaitax_calc.py`; its rates live in `scripts/rates.json`.

## Individuals

- Progressive rates on net income (verified on rd.go.th, Revenue Code as amended by Act No. 44, 2017): 0% up to THB 150,000 · 5% to 300,000 · 10% to 500,000 · 15% to 750,000 · 20% to 1,000,000 · 25% to 2,000,000 · 30% to 5,000,000 · 35% above 5,000,000.
- A person in Thailand 180 days or more in a calendar year is tax resident.
- Annual return: PND.90 (several income types, including foreign income) or PND.91 (Thai salary only), due 31 March, about a week later online. PND.94 half-year return by 30 September for non-salary income such as rent.

## Foreign income and remittance (individuals)

Status as compiled in November 2026. **Verify every run.**

- Thailand taxes a **resident's** foreign-source income on a **remittance basis**: only what is brought into Thailand.
- **Por.161/2566** (from 1 Jan 2024): foreign income earned from 2024 by a person who was resident in the year it arose is assessable in the year it is brought in, whatever year that is.
- **Por.162/2566**: income earned **before 1 Jan 2024** keeps the old treatment and is generally not taxed when brought in later. This needs proof of when it was earned.
- **Non-residents** (under 180 days) are not taxed on foreign income brought in. Thai-source income is always taxable, including pay for work done in Thailand that lands in a foreign account.
- A **draft** relief (foreign income brought in during the year it was earned or the following year would be exempt) was announced in 2025. It stalled with the December 2025 dissolution of parliament and was **not law** as of autumn 2026. Never plan on it.
- Spending on a foreign card in Thailand, and ATM withdrawals, are treated in practice as bringing money in. The guidance is unsettled, so flag these.
- Double-tax treaties can give a credit for foreign tax paid. The Revenue Department introduced a foreign-tax-credit tool and a separate declaration for foreign-source income in 2026. Check their current status.
- Some LTR visa categories exempt foreign income brought in. DTV carries no known tax exemption. Flag; never apply.
- A worldwide-taxation proposal was discussed and set aside. Re-check.
