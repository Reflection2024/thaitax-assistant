FAQ

Questions you might have

คำถามที่พบบ่อย

Is it really free?

Yes. Sabai Office is free to download and use. You need your own Claude plan, plus FlowAccount or Stripe if you want live connections.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It does the preparation, so your accountant reviews a clean file instead of a box of receipts. Filing stays with you and your accountant.

I don't use FlowAccount. Can I still use it?

Yes. Upload the export from PEAK, Express, your gateway or your bank, and Sabai Office reads it.

I only have personal income. Is it for me?

Yes. Freelance work, rent from a property, or salary with side income: Sabai Office prepares your personal tax file and keeps track of your withholding certificates.

I live here, but my income comes from abroad. Do I need to file?

If you spend 180 days or more in Thailand in a year and bring income earned from 2024 into the country, it is likely reportable. Sabai Office checks your days and your transfers, prepares the foreign-income declaration and the treaty credit, and lists what your accountant needs to confirm.

Where does my data go?

Your data stays between you, Claude and the services you connect. Sabai Office does not send ID numbers, salaries or bank details to any outside service.
