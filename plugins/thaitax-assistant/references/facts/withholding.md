## Withholding tax (ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย)

The **payer** withholds, on the amount before VAT, then remits it and gives the payee a certificate (50 ทวิ).

| Payment to a Thai resident | Usual rate |
|---|---|
| Services, professional fees, contractors, commission | 3% |
| Rent of property | 5% |
| Advertising | 2% |
| Transport (not public passenger) | 1% |
| Non-life insurance premium | 1% |
| Prizes, promotions | 5% |
| Dividends | 10% |
| Salaries | progressive table, PND.1 |

- **Which form depends on who is paid.** PND.1 salaries · PND.3 Thai individuals · PND.53 Thai companies and partnerships · PND.54 non-residents (often 15%; a double-tax treaty may lower it) · PND.2 dividends or interest paid to individuals.
- Identify the payee's legal form before choosing the form. A certificate issued on the wrong form cannot be used by the payee.
- **e-Withholding:** the 5%, 3% and 2% rates drop to 1% when the payment goes through the banks' e-WHT system, for payments from 1 Jan 2026 to **31 Dec 2027** (Ministerial Regulation No. 402 B.E. 2569, gazetted October 2026, retroactive to 1 Jan 2026; cabinet approval 16 Jun 2026). Apply this only if the payer actually uses e-WHT. Payments made in 2026 before the regulation was gazetted may have been withheld at the normal rate; ask the accountant how to treat them.
- **Deadlines:** PND.1, 2, 3, 53 and 54 are due the 7th on paper and the 15th online. **The current online 8-day extension (MoF notice of 12 Jan 2024) covers returns due 1 Feb 2024 to 31 Jan 2027.** A renewal had not been published as of 11 Nov 2026 (earlier renewals came out in mid-January). For any return due after 31 Jan 2027, confirm the renewal before quoting an online deadline; without it, the paper deadline applies.
- No withholding on water, electricity or public transport.
- A withholding the payer missed is the payer's liability. List it; never ignore it.
