# Company housekeeping and visa reminders: dates only

Last verified: 2026-11-11. These are **reminder** facts, not advice. Verify each one with `../research-protocol.md` (dbd.go.th, immigration.go.th, doe.go.th / mol.go.th) before setting a date the user relies on. Immigration practice varies by office (Koh Samui and Surat Thani offices differ), so tell the user to confirm with their office or agent.

## Thai limited company: the yearly cycle (Civil and Commercial Code, DBD)

| What | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Financial year end | per the company's registration | usually 31 Dec |
| Audit of the financial statements | before the AGM | by a licensed Thai auditor (CPA) |
| **Annual general meeting (AGM)** | within **4 months** of year end | notice to shareholders 7 days before (more for some resolutions); minutes kept |
| **Financial statements to DBD** (ส.บช.3 via DBD e-Filing) | within **1 month** of the AGM | late filing means fines for the company and directors |
| **Shareholder list (บอจ.5)** | within **14 days** of the AGM | also after any share transfer |
| **PND.50** | within 150 days of year end | separate from DBD |
| **PND.51** | within 2 months after month 6 | |
| Changes: directors, authorised signatories, address, objectives, capital | register within **14 days** of the resolution | at DBD; some need a special resolution |
| Company sign at the office | always | Thai name; required |
| Share register, minutes book, company seal (if any) | kept up to date | |
| Licences: hotel, food, tourism, BOI reports | per licence | list each with its renewal date from the user |

Foreign-owned or BOI companies have extra reporting (BOI progress reports, foreign capital). Record the dates the user gives; never assess eligibility.

## Visa and work-permit reminders (individual)

| What | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **90-day report** (TM47) | every 90 days in Thailand; **in person or by agent:** 15 days before to 7 days after the due date; **online:** from 15 days before, closing on or a few days before the due date (offices differ); **by post:** posted at least 15 days before; fine THB 2,000 if late | first report in person; the clock resets on re-entry |
| **Extension of stay** | apply before the permission to stay expires (about 30–45 days before is common) | documents depend on the visa type (marriage, retirement, business, education) |
| **Re-entry permit** | before leaving Thailand, if the user wants to keep the extension | single or multiple |
| **Work permit** | renew before expiry; it is tied to the employer and the role | change of employer or role means a new permit |
| **TM30** (address notification) | the landlord or house owner reports within 24 hours of a foreigner's arrival at the address | on hotels, rentals, and returns from abroad; usually the owner's duty |
| **LTR visa** | annual report instead of 90-day reports | LTR holders get specific tax treatment; see `individuals.md` |
| Passport expiry | at least 6–12 months before | a visa cannot run past it |

Never give immigration advice beyond these dates and lists. Anything about eligibility, overstays or status goes to "for your visa agent or lawyer".
