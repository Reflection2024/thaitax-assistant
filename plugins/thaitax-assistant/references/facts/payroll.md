# Payroll and social security

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Re-verify before use (see `_index.md`). For arithmetic use `scripts/thaitax_calc.py`; its rates live in `scripts/rates.json`.

## Social security (ประกันสังคม)

- 5% employee and 5% employer, on monthly wages up to a ceiling of **THB 17,500** from 1 Jan 2026 (THB 875 maximum each side), with a floor of THB 1,650. The ceiling rises in 2029 and again in 2032.
- Form SPS 1-10 and payment are due the **15th** of the next month. Late payment carries a 2% surcharge per month.
- Register a new employee within 30 days of hire. Foreign staff with a work permit are covered.

## Employee Welfare Fund (กองทุนสงเคราะห์ลูกจ้าง)

- New contribution for employers with **10 or more employees** that have no qualifying registered provident fund or employer welfare fund.
- **Start: 1 Jan 2027** (postponed from 1 Oct 2026 by cabinet in October 2026, called a final deferral). **0.25% employee + 0.25% employer** of wages to 31 Dec 2031, then 0.5% each from 1 Jan 2032. Due by the 15th of the next month.
- **Unverified:** the three implementing regulations had not been published in the Royal Gazette as of 11 Nov 2026. Confirm the start date, wage base and due date before the January 2027 payroll.

## Payroll

- The minimum wage depends on the province and sometimes the district. Current instrument: Wage Committee Notification No. 14, in force from 1 Jul 2025. Verify the current table for the exact district before using it:
  - **Koh Samui district: THB 400/day.**
  - **Rest of Surat Thani, including Koh Phangan: THB 352/day.** Koh Phangan falls in the "rest of province" band; no official table naming it was read, so mark it unverified.
  - Hotels (Hotel Act types 2–4) and licensed entertainment venues: THB 400/day under the July 2025 change. Its exact provincial scope was not confirmed in this run; check before applying it in Surat Thani.
  - The **2027 review** is under way: provincial subcommittees submitted proposals by 5 Nov 2026; no new rates or start date had been announced on 11 Nov 2026.
- **Overtime:** 1.5x on a working day. Holiday work is 1x extra for staff entitled to holiday pay, otherwise 2x. Overtime on a holiday is 3x. Hourly rate = monthly wage ÷ (30 × normal daily hours).
- **Severance** follows service tiers under the Labour Protection Act. Hand it to a lawyer; never compute a payment instruction.
- A foreigner needs a valid work permit for the employer and the role. Do not stage pay for anyone whose permit status is unknown.
