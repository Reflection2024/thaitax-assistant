# Thai tax, payroll and calendar facts

Read this before any Thai amount, rate or deadline goes into a deliverable.

**Facts change.** The figures below were compiled in November 2026. Before the owner acts on a rate or a deadline, check it against an official source (rd.go.th for the Revenue Department, sso.go.th for social security, mol.go.th for the minimum wage, the Royal Gazette) with web search. Write "verified on <date>, <source>" next to it. If the check fails, mark the figure **unverified**.

Every deliverable carries this line:
- EN: *Prepared for review by your Thai accountant. Not tax or legal advice. Nothing has been filed.*
- HE: *הוכן לבדיקת רואה החשבון התאילנדי שלך. אינו ייעוץ מס או ייעוץ משפטי. דבר לא הוגש.*
- TH: *จัดเตรียมเพื่อให้นักบัญชีของท่านตรวจสอบ ไม่ใช่คำแนะนำด้านภาษีหรือกฎหมาย และยังไม่มีการยื่นแบบใด ๆ*

## VAT (ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม)

- Rate **7%** (a royal decree reduces the statutory 10%). The current decree runs to **30 Sep 2027**. Check the rate for any document dated after that.
- Registration is mandatory above THB 1.8 million of VAT-able annual revenue. A business that is not registered cannot charge VAT or issue a tax invoice.
- **PP.30 (ภ.พ.30)** is filed every month, including months with no sales. Due the 15th of the next month on paper, the 23rd online.
- **PP.36 (ภ.พ.36)** is reverse-charge VAT: self-assess 7% on services bought from a foreign provider and used in Thailand (Google Ads, Meta, AWS, SaaS). Due the 15th. The input credit is usually claimable.
- Input VAT can be claimed only with a valid tax invoice that carries the company's exact registered name, address and tax ID. Entertainment, passenger cars under 10 seats, and invoices missing a mandatory field cannot be claimed.
- Exports and services used abroad are 0%. Education, healthcare, residential rent and some land sales are exempt or fall under other taxes. Classify each item; never assume.

## Tax invoice (ใบกำกับภาษี): mandatory content

- The words "Tax Invoice / ใบกำกับภาษี".
- Seller: name, address, 13-digit tax ID, branch ("สำนักงานใหญ่" or a 5-digit branch code).
- Buyer: name and address, plus tax ID and branch if the buyer is VAT-registered.
- A running serial number from the issuer's system, and the date.
- Description, quantity and value of what was sold.
- VAT on its own line.

The invoice is issued at the tax point: payment for services, delivery for goods. e-Tax invoices are optional for most businesses.

## Withholding tax (ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย)

The **payer** withholds, on the amount before VAT, then remits it and gives the payee a certificate (50 ทวิ).

| Payment to a Thai resident | Usual rate |
|---|---|
| Services, professional fees, contractors, commission | 3% |
| Rent of property | 5% |
| Advertising | 2% |
| Transport (not public passenger) | 1% |
| Non-life insurance premium | 1% |
| Prizes, promotions | 5% |
| Dividends | 10% |
| Salaries | progressive table, PND.1 |

- **Which form depends on who is paid.** PND.1 salaries · PND.3 Thai individuals · PND.53 Thai companies and partnerships · PND.54 non-residents (often 15%; a double-tax treaty may lower it) · PND.2 dividends or interest paid to individuals.
- Identify the payee's legal form before choosing the form. A certificate issued on the wrong form cannot be used by the payee.
- **e-Withholding:** the 5%, 3% and 2% rates drop to 1% when the payment goes through the banks' e-WHT system (extended to 31 Dec 2027). Apply this only if the payer actually uses e-WHT.
- **Deadlines:** PND.1, 2, 3, 53 and 54 are due the 7th on paper and the 15th online. The online extension is renewed by Ministry of Finance notice, so confirm it is still in force.
- No withholding on water, electricity or public transport.
- A withholding the payer missed is the payer's liability. List it; never ignore it.

## Corporate income tax

- 20% standard.
- **SME rate** (paid-up capital up to THB 5m **and** revenue up to THB 30m): 0% on the first THB 300,000 of profit, 15% up to THB 3m, 20% above. The accountant confirms SME status.
- **PND.51** half-year return: within 2 months after the first six months (31 Aug for a calendar year).
- **PND.50** annual return: within 150 days of year end (around 30 May for a 31 Dec year end; online gets 8 more days).
- Audited financial statements go to the DBD through DBD e-Filing, within one month of the shareholders' meeting. The meeting is held within 4 months of year end.
- The Foreign Business Act, the US Treaty of Amity and BOI status are legal questions. Flag them to a lawyer; never decide them inside a tax deliverable.

## Individuals

- Progressive rates on net income: 0% up to THB 150k; 5% / 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% in the bands up to THB 2m; 30% and 35% above that. Verify the top bands.
- A person in Thailand 180 days or more in a calendar year is tax resident.
- From 2024, a resident's foreign income is taxable when it is brought into Thailand. Flag this for a specialist; never calculate it.

## Social security (ประกันสังคม)

- 5% employee and 5% employer, on monthly wages up to a ceiling of **THB 17,500** from 1 Jan 2026 (THB 875 maximum each side), with a floor of THB 1,650. The ceiling rises in 2029 and again in 2032.
- Form SPS 1-10 and payment are due the **15th** of the next month. Late payment carries a 2% surcharge per month.
- Register a new employee within 30 days of hire. Foreign staff with a work permit are covered.

## Payroll

- The minimum wage depends on the province and sometimes the district. Verify the current table for the exact district before using it (Koh Samui and Koh Phangan have different rates).
- **Overtime:** 1.5x on a working day. Holiday work is 1x extra for staff entitled to holiday pay, otherwise 2x. Overtime on a holiday is 3x. Hourly rate = monthly wage ÷ (30 × normal daily hours).
- **Severance** follows service tiers under the Labour Protection Act. Hand it to a lawyer; never compute a payment instruction.
- A foreigner needs a valid work permit for the employer and the role. Do not stage pay for anyone whose permit status is unknown.

## Calendar

- Official Thai documents use the Buddhist Era: **BE = CE + 543** (2026 = 2569). Convert, and say that you did.
- A deadline that falls on a weekend or public holiday moves to the next working day. Check the current year's holiday list.
- The financial year is usually the calendar year, but read it from the profile.
